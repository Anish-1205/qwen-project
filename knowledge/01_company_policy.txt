ACME NORTHSTAR — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: January 15, 2026

REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests should be submitted at least two business days before the requested remote day.

Employees working remotely must remain available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time. Meetings scheduled during those hours take priority over individual work.

ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests longer than five consecutive business days require approval from the employee's department director.

SICK LEAVE

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not require advance approval, but employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

EQUIPMENT

The company provides one standard laptop and one external monitor to eligible remote employees. Additional equipment requires approval from the IT department.

SECURITY

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption, automatic screen locking after 10 minutes of inactivity, and multi-factor authentication.

Questions about this policy should be directed to the People Operations team.
