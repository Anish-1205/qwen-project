NORTHSTAR X200 DEVICE — USER MANUAL
Model: X200
Revision: 4

POWER

The X200 requires a 65-watt USB-C power adapter. The device can operate while charging.

BATTERY

A fully charged battery provides approximately 11 hours of typical mixed-use operation. Battery life may be lower under sustained high CPU usage.

RESET PROCEDURE

To perform a soft reset, hold the power button for 8 seconds and release it. Wait approximately 15 seconds before turning the device on again.

If the soft reset does not resolve the problem, disconnect all external devices and hold the power button for 15 seconds.

CONNECTIVITY

The X200 supports Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3. The device supports up to two external displays through compatible USB-C or docking hardware.

STORAGE

The standard X200 configuration includes a 512 GB NVMe SSD. Storage is not user-replaceable.

WARNING

Do not open the device enclosure while the device is connected to power. Internal components should only be serviced by authorized technicians.
